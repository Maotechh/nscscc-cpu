# Hierarchical TLB timing iteration

Date: 2026-07-25

## Objective

Reduce complete-SoC routing congestion and the long translation paths caused by two parallel
32-entry TLB CAM ports. Preserve the fixed fetch4/issue4/decode3/commit3 architecture, LA32R TLB
management semantics, and the automatically generated Verilog-only implementation policy.

## Design

- Each instruction/data translation port owns a four-entry positive MicroTLB and one negative
  miss entry. A warm hit or repeated miss returns one cycle after request acceptance.
- One round-robin MainTLB walker scans four entries per cycle. The 32 architectural entries are
  physically expressed as four banks by eight rows, so each walker lane selects one 8:1 row
  rather than generating four independent 32:1 bundle muxes.
- A cold MainTLB lookup completes after at most eight scan cycles. Writes, fills, and INVTLB clear
  both MicroTLBs, both negative entries, and any in-flight walk before the architectural redirect.
- TLBSRCH remains combinational at the privileged retirement boundary. TLB read/write, global,
  ASID, 4 KiB/4 MiB page selection, dirty, valid, privilege, and memory-attribute behavior remain
  equivalent to the previous architectural storage.

## Structural checks

The initial flat `Vec(32)` walker was rejected before Vivado because Yosys expanded bundle
selection into 2,922 equality cells. The banked layout reduces this to 1,362 equality cells. The
generated complete RTL is 5,562,959 bytes with SHA-256
`cc05e3020320d8dc6b88b28b752e6e6d7dee2e6a1f4bce8d3dc785ac505fb5d5`.

## Compliance and limits

All CPU behavior changes are Scala/SpinalHDL source; `rtl/mycpu_top.v` is generated by the locked
publisher. No timing exception, false path, simulator shortcut, handwritten production Verilog,
or workload-specific behavior was added. Standalone synthesis and one `func_lab19` run do not
prove complete-SoC place/route timing, remote `func58`, or `perf20`; those remain commit-bound
promotion gates for this candidate.
